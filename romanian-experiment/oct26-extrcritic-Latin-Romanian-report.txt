--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Source: Pardess_From_Latin_to_Romanian_Phonology.pdf. Every rule's prose,
  formula and examples were read in the OCR text layer. Where the OCR was
  ambiguous, the page scan itself was viewed (R2A, R13A, R33, R10Ca examples).
  The OCR garbles IPA badly (ɛ, ə, ŋ, ʎ), so no symbol claim rests on OCR alone.
- Format: DiaSim (Research/DiaSim, gamma; symbolDefs.csv; -diacrit with the
  default diacritics file).
  (a) The extraction was run as a whole on RoLLex: it parses and simulates with
      no errors.
  (b) Every suspected defect, and every proposed fix, was run as a single-rule
      cascade against a small test lexicon of input/expected-output pairs.
  (c) The corrected cascade was re-run as a whole. That run caught one error in
      my own first draft of the R14 fix ("{e;ˌe} > ∅" is malformed; it must be
      "{e;ˌe} > {∅;∅}"), which was corrected before delivery.
- Output resolution: feature-changing outputs were checked against the
  symbolDefs.csv rows (e.g. R2A a > ɛː, R2B o > eː, R10Ca k > p and ŋ > m,
  R19 t͡ʃ > t͡s, R44 ə > e all resolve to defined symbols).
- Page citations: each rule's printed page span was computed from the page
  numbers in the OCR, and the irregular cases were checked by hand.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERALL ASSESSMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This extraction is much better than the July one. It handles correctly nearly
every error class from the user's July/June gradings:
- glides as separate segments (a j, e̯ a)
- Pardess's y = j
- "//" split into two rules
- alpha features with one variable per feature
- no feature matrices aligned to ∅ (phone lists in R18A, insertion + deletion
  for metatheses)
- [lo], not [low]
- ∅, not 0
- no "C" shorthand and no empty contexts
- outputs completed to reach defined phones (D9)
- central ə/ɨ written as symbols (D6)
- nasals-as-stops written as [αcont,αnas] (D7)
- Pardess's [rep] rendered as the ɾ/r contrast
- negative contexts written as positive disjunctions (R31A, R59A)
- R52B kept to the voiced affricate; R46A covering the glide e̯
- every justified departure from Pardess carried in a NOTE

No rule had a reversed environment, a wrong trigger segment, or a hallucinated
change.

What remains: one systemic mistranslation (stress, Part A1), the missing
synoptic-index page citations (Part A2), five rule-level formalism defects
(Part B), and three places that needed a note (Part C).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART A. SYSTEMIC ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1. [-acc] rendered as [-stres] instead of [-prim]  (Executive Decision D4)
    -- SOURCE FIDELITY, output-changing.
    D4 rightly sets Pardess's [+acc] = [+prim]. Pardess's own abbreviation key
    defines [acc] as primary stress, and his system has no secondary stress.
    But D4 then sets [-acc] = [-stres], and treats the plain vowel symbols
    (a, e, i, o, u) as "unstressed". In DiaSim a secondary-stressed vowel (ˌV)
    is [+stres,-prim], so every rule for Pardess's "unstressed" vowels silently
    exempted ˌV. Pardess would class such a vowel as [-acc]. RoLLex marks
    secondary stress often (Latin column: ˌa x186, ˌi x82, ˌe x76, ˌo x42,
    ˌu x33).
    DiaSim evidence, using the extraction's own lines:
      R13D  ˌi ˈa  stays ˌi ˈa                     (expected j ˈa)
      R28   ˌoɾɾésko  keeps ˌo                     (expected ˌu)
      R37B  ˌa ˈa  stays ˌa ˈa                     (expected ˈa)
      R39   t͡ʃˌitáte  keeps ˌi                     (expected ˌe)
    CORRECTED in D4 and in 11 rules: R4A, R13B, R13D, R14, R28, R35A, R36A,
    R37A, R37B, R38, R39.
    - Feature rules: [-stres] -> [-prim].
    - Symbol rules: the ˌ-variant added to the disjunction.
    Every corrected line was DiaSim-tested.

A2. Synoptic-index pages missing from every $$PG: citation  -- PAGES.
    Pardess lists every rule again in the Ch.8 synoptic index (pp.137-141).
    The extraction cites only the body pages. (This is the "global" page
    problem in the user's June 2026 annotation.)
    CORRECTED for all rules, comment-only entries included:
      R1-R5A    p.137
      R5B-R18A  p.138   (p.138 also lists the body's R13G as "R13F", as the
                         extraction itself notes)
      R18B-R29A p.139
      R29B-R45B p.140
      R46A-R60  p.141
    Two body-page ranges were also short:
      R22E  "77"  -> "77-78"   (its derivation table is on p.78)
      R25B  "81"  -> "81-82"   (the examples, incl. the plural restorations,
                                are on p.82)
    All other body-page citations were checked and are correct.

PART B. RULE-LEVEL FORMALISM ERRORS (corrected)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R5C (p.53) -- CONTEXT / SOURCE FIDELITY.
    The extraction added a prior context "[+cons] __" with no note. Pardess's
    rule is just "V r # > r V #".
    FIX: context removed  ->  ∅ [+syl] ɾ > ɾ [+syl] ∅ / __ #
    (tested: supeɾ > supɾe, inteɾ > intɾe).

R13C (p.63) -- INPUT / TARGET FORMAT, output-changing. Two defects.
    (1) The identity matrix (αhi,βlo,γback,δfront,εround,ζlong) omits [tense],
        the only feature separating e from ɛ in symbolDefs.
        Tested: ɛː eː (ɛː from R2A) wrongly contracted.
    (2) The line always deletes the second vowel, so where the second member is
        stressed the stress is lost.
        Tested: u ˈu > u.
    FIX: ηtense added. The non-primary-stressed member is deleted instead, in
    two lines (second member, then first), as the extraction already does for
    R37B. Tested: mórtuu > mórtu, u ˈu > ˈu, ɛː eː kept.

R37B (p.103) -- INPUT / TARGET FORMAT, output-changing. Two defects.
    (1) [-stres] (see A1): ˌa ˈa was not degeminated.
    (2) [tense] missing from the identity matrix, as in R13C.
        Tested: ɛ e wrongly contracted.
    FIX: [-prim]; ζtense added. Tested: kásaa > kása, kaálu > kálu,
    beére > bére, ˌa ˈa > ˈa, ɛ e kept, and ə ə > ə (DiaSim handles ə's
    tense value 0 correctly in alpha matching).

R43 (p.108) -- CONTEXT / SOURCE FIDELITY.
    Pardess's context is [-cont,-ant,-cor]. The extraction replaced it, with no
    note, by [+cons,-son,-ant,-cor]. That class also admits the velar and
    palatal fricatives x, ɣ, ç, which Pardess's [-cont] excludes.
    FIX: Pardess's matrix restored  ->  l > ʎ / [-cont,-ant,-cor] __
    (In DiaSim this picks out k, ɡ, c, ɟ. A note records that Pardess's [-cont]
    would also include ŋ, which DiaSim codes [+cont]; no ŋl case is attested.)

R53B (pp.117-118) -- CONTEXT / SOURCE FIDELITY.
    Pardess: "ea > e / __ $ Co e". The extraction rendered Co as ([+cons])*,
    which lets a hiatus e̯a e (no consonant) monophthongize too. Every one of
    Pardess's seven examples has at least one consonant after the syllable
    boundary.
    FIX: ([+cons])+, as in the user's own June 2026 gold annotation for this
    rule. Tested: me̯áse > mése, ve̯áde > véde, e̯ˈa e left alone.
    D8 is annotated with this exception.

R4A, R13B, R13D, R14, R28, R35A, R36A, R37A, R38, R39
    -- the stress mistranslation of A1; corrected as described there.

     His formula ([+high], "usually post-tonic") does not cover it, and neither

     dómina > dómna.)
R31A The extraction notes that the formula fails to except dénte. The
     opposite conflict is unnoted: Pardess's own R31B example dómna > doámna
     (p.96), and his p.97 derivation running domna through R31A, require an
     "mC" form (mn) to undergo R31A, which his exception forbids. The lines

     In fact his matrix [-cont,+high,-str] also covers the velar stops k, ɡ.
     The restriction to ʎ, ɲ follows his title and examples (justifiable:
     mikə stays). The note is qualified to say this is a departure.

Points worth recording:
- R10Ca: Pardess writes the nasal examples with eng (díŋnu > dímnu, verified on
  the scan), so carrying the velar's OWN nasality ([αcont,αnas]) is faithful.
- R22D before R24 is correct: Pardess (pp.76-77) uses úβer > úger > údʒer
  (DR uger [ˈudʒer]) precisely to order R22D before R24. The July critic's
  "correction", which swapped these two rules, was itself an error; this
  extraction's order should stand.
- R24's skio > ščio: the s > ʃ is supplied later by R45A, so there is no gap.
- R46A line (ii): the extraction's reading of the glide's "following syllable"
  as the one after its a nucleus is what makes Pardess's listed exceptions
  (ve̯áde, me̯áse, pe̯ánne, be̯áre) come out right.
- R59A: the four positive contexts exhaust "X is not C + liquid" (a preceding
  vowel is excluded, as the Ch.7 derivation requires). The j̥ symbol the
  extraction asked to have verified resolves under -diacrit (DiaSim defines
  "j̥" from j + ̥ = [-voi]).
- Departures that are disclosed in notes and that I accept as justified from
  Pardess's own derivations or examples:
    R4B-ii (∅ > k / s __ l, from prose); R6 (restricted to prevocalic, non-velar
    w); R11-ii (nasal assimilation, from lingua > limbwa); R13A (front first V,
    mid second V); R15 (no pairs with a); R19 (([+cons])* before the affricate);
    R20 (labial Co, yod only); R27B (yod insertion + tensing in one line);
    R31B (both steps in one line); R40 ([+cons] before i); R52B (voiced only).

PART E. DIASIM RUN RESULTS AND THE LEXICON INTERFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both the extraction and the corrected file parse and run on RoLLex.
Exact-match accuracy against the RoLLex gold:
                                          extraction   corrected
  RoLLex as is                              19.27%       19.41%
  avg. feature edit distance                 0.356        0.344
  Latin column converted to D1/D2 (*)        19.84%       19.99%
  ... and ɾ read as r, secondary stress
      ignored, on both sides                   --         43.4%
  (*) r -> ɾ, AE/OE glide e̯ -> j, ɡ n -> ŋ n; scratch copy only.

The low raw score is not mainly a fidelity problem:
- Input side: RoLLex's Latin column does not follow D1/D2. So R2A/B, R5C,
  R10A, R10Ca (gn), R26A/B and the ɾ-dependent rules cannot fire as Pardess
  intends.
- Output side: the RoLLex Romanian column writes the rhotic as r and has no
  secondary stress, while the cascade (faithfully: R51 trill > flap) ends in ɾ
  and never removes ˌV. Every result containing ɾ or ˌV misses.
- The rest: sporadic or lexical rules encoded as general ones (D10; e.g. R10A
  turns ursu > us), and rules Pardess's text does not supply.
These are cascade/lexicon conventions for the user to decide on. They were not
changed in the corrected file, since they are not unfaithful to Pardess. A
comment in the header records them.

PART F. FLAGGED, NOT CORRECTED (with reasons)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Co" in general (D8 = ([+cons])*): only R53B was changed, because there the
  examples and the user's June annotation are explicit. Whether Pardess's Co
  should otherwise be * or + is an open question for the user (the June gold
  uses + in R20, R31A, R46A, R53B; the July notes say *). In this extraction,
  R31A and R20 already require at least one consonant by construction, and
  R46A uses *.
- R31A domna and dénte: the formula is followed, and both conflicts are noted.
  Fixing them would mean choosing between Pardess's formula and his examples.
- R59A restricts devoicing to unstressed u, i; Pardess's formula has no stress
  condition. Disclosed by the extractor; harmless (no stressed final high
  vowels in his data).
- Sporadic and lexical rules (R4A, R10A, R22D, R30, R53A) are encoded as
  general rules, per D10.
