$ EXECUTIVE DECISIONS
$ E1. PIE input conventions follow Clayton's DiaCLEA18 (Albanian-CFR): h1 = h, h2 = χ, h3 = xʷ;
$     i̯ u̯ = j w; palatovelars k̑ g̑ g̑h = c ɟ ɟʰ; labiovelars kʷ ɡʷ ɡʷʰ; syllabic resonants r̩ l̩ m̩ n̩.
$     Input forms are UNACCENTED: PIE accent and the BS stress laws (Hirt's, de Saussure's, Dybo's Laws;
$     accentual paradigms, metatony; pp. 1980-1983) are not modelled.
$ E2. The BS acute (Kim: "acuteness is better understood as a privative feature ... The main such property
$     was probably glottalization", p. 1980) is encoded as [+cg] on the syllable nucleus, written ˀ
$     (e.g. aːˀ, iˀ r). Circumflex/nonacute = plain. On an acute diphthong of vowel + sonorant, ˀ sits on
$     the vowel.
$ E3. ś ź (PBS reflexes of the palatals, "probably alveopalatal") = ɕ ʑ; the ruki product š = ʃ.
$ E4. Lithuanian ie / uo are written as two segments (i e, u o); Lithuanian ė = eː, y = iː, ū = uː.
$     Lithuanian intonation is a prosodic accent, not segmental glottalization, so [+cg] is removed at the
$     end (K-Lith-acc).

$ K3a Winter's Law: short vowels lengthened (with acute) before PIE voiced unaspirated stops  $$PG: 1978
$ "PIE short vowels were lengthened in pre-PBS when immediately followed by voiced unaspirated stops, but
$ not when followed by voiced aspirates" (*ud-r-eh2 > *ū́drā́-, *nogwo- > *nṓgas vs. *nébhos > *nebas,
$ *médhu > *medu). Exceptions: *wod-r̥ ~ *wed-n- > Lith. vanduõ (no lengthening).
$ NOTE: the lengthened vowel is acute (Kim's PBS forms *ū́-, *ṓ), hence [+cg]; an already long vowel
$ (*h1ḗd-ti > *ḗsti) also becomes acute. "Widespread disagreement over the precise conditioning" -
$ TENTATIVE.
$ ORDER: before K2b (merger of aspirates, which would destroy the conditioning) and before K2a
$ (voicing assimilation: *h1ḗd-ti still has d).
[+syl] > [+long,+cg] / __ [-son,-cont,+voi,-sg]

$ K2b Merger of PIE voiced and voiced aspirated stops  $$PG: 1975
$ "The PIE stops traditionally labeled 'voiced' and 'voiced aspirated' merged as voiced stops".
[-son,+voi,+sg] > [-sg]

$ K2c Merger of velars and labiovelars  $$PG: 1975
$ "The PIE velar and labiovelar stops merged in PBS" (*kwós > *kas; *gwén- > *genā́).
{kʷ;ɡʷ} > {k;ɡ}

$ K2d Palatals > alveopalatal sibilants *ś, *ź  $$PG: 1975
$ "The PIE palatal stops *k̑ and *g̑, *g̑h developed into anterior sibilants, probably alveopalatal".
$ NOTE: the "Gutturalwechsel" (velars for PIE palatals, e.g. Lith. akmuõ beside ašmuõ, klausýti) is
$ dialectal/lexical - not encoded.
{c;ɟ} > {ɕ;ʑ}

$ K2e The ruki rule: *s > *š after *i, *u, *k, *r  $$PG: 1976
$ "*s was retracted (probably to a palatoalveolar sibilant, here denoted *š) when preceded by *i, *u, *k,
$ or *r" - consistent in Slavic, "much less regular in Lithuanian". Encoded at PBS level.
$ NOTE: i/u include their non-syllabic variants j/w ([+hi,-cons]); k includes k < kʷ (after K2c).
$ NOTE: NOT applied word-finally: all of Kim's own PBS forms keep final *-s after i, u (*patis, *mirti-,
$ *sūnus, *źwēri-), and DiaSim testing with them confirmed the unrestricted rule contradicts them.
s > ʃ / {[+hi,-cons];k;r} __ {[+syl];[-syl]}

$ K3f Syllabic resonants > *iR  $$PG: 1978
$ "The PIE syllabic sonorants *r̥, *l̥, *m̥, *n̥ became *iR in most cases" (*mr̥-ti- > *mirti-; *wl̥kwos >
$ *wilkas; *k̑m̥tóm > *śimtan; *mn̥-ti- > *minti-). The less frequent *uR (expressive words: kum̃pas,
$ pur̃vas) is LEXICAL - not encoded.
$ ORDER: before K3e (so that *pl̥h1-nó- > *pílna- shows the VRH > V́R acute).
{r̩;l̩;m̩;n̩} > {i r;i l;i m;i n}

$ K3b Word-initial laryngeals disappear  $$PG: 1977
$ "Word-initial laryngeals disappeared without reflex" (*h3bhrúHs >→ *bruwi-).
{h;χ;xʷ} > {∅;∅;∅} / # __

$ K3c Intervocalic laryngeals: *iHV, *uHV > *ijV, *uwV; otherwise lost, then contraction  $$PG: 1977, 1981
$ "Intervocalic laryngeals were also lost, and the resulting sequence of vowels underwent contraction, except
$ that *iHV, *uHV > *ijV, *uwV (Smoczyński 2003)"; "sequences *VHV contracted to circumflex long vowels
$ and diphthongs in PBS" (*-eh2-es > *-ās).
$ NOTE: the contracted vowel is circumflex (no [+cg]). Its quality: Kim's examples have a as one member
$ (*-eh2-es > *-ās; o-stem abl. *-e-ad > *-ā), so (iii) a low second vowel absorbs the first, (iv)
$ otherwise the first vowel is kept and lengthened - TENTATIVE.

{h;χ;xʷ} > {w;w;w} / [+syl,+hi,+back] __ [+syl]
{h;χ;xʷ} > {∅;∅;∅} / [+syl] __ [+syl]
[+syl,-lo] [+syl,+lo] > ∅ [+long]
[+syl] [+syl] > [+long] ∅

$ K3d Laryngeal between obstruents in an initial syllable > *a  $$PG: 1977

$ K3e Vowel + laryngeal before a consonant or # > acute long vowel; VRH > acute VR  $$PG: 1977, 1981

$ "PBS inherited the PIE rule of regressive voicing assimilation" (*deg-téi > Lith. dègti [-kt-]; *duktē);

$ K3g Post-PIE *o and *a merge as *a  $$PG: 1977
